# Page 2 — Meet Eve, the Attacker

To make this easier to follow, let's meet our cast:

| Character | Role |
|---|---|
| **Alice** | The software developer. She builds updates and publishes them. |
| **Bob** | The user. He just wants to safely update his software. |
| **Eve** | The attacker. She wants to trick Bob into installing something bad. |

```
Alice  →  publishes an update
Eve    →  tries to intercept or replace it
Bob    →  downloads *something* — but is it really Alice's?
```

Every attack in this story follows the same shape: **Eve gets between Alice and Bob**, and tries to make Bob trust something he shouldn't.

Keep this trio in mind — everything from here on is really just "how does Bob outsmart Eve?"
